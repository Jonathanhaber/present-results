Definitions and data

Trial
A free 14-day trial started between 1 Jul and 30 Sep 2026, one per account.
Finished trial
A trial with a final outcome on 3 Oct 2026: converted, cancelled or expired.
Still open
A trial with no outcome yet on 3 Oct 2026: still running, or ran out less than 7 days before without a payment so far. It could still convert, so it is not counted in any rate.
Converted
The account paid for the first time during the trial or within 7 days after it.
Cancelled
The user ended the trial early, without paying.
Expired
The trial ran out and no payment followed within 7 days.
Conversion rate
Converted ÷ finished trials.
Time to convert
Trial start to first payment; median of converted trials.
Channel
The first-touch acquisition channel of the account.
Small n
Fewer than 10 finished trials. The rate is shown muted and left out of rankings.
Source
billing.trials joined with billing.payments, pulled 3 Oct 2026, 09:00 CEST.
